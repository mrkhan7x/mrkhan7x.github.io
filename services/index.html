Common questions.

Who owns the system and data once built?+

You retain 100% ownership of every workflow, database schema, prompt, and custom script. At final handover, you receive full administrative access, source files, and operational documentation. Nothing runs on proprietary locks.

How does pricing work?+

Every project has a clear, fixed price quote provided after the 30-minute operational audit. There are no surprise hourly rates or hidden maintenance fees.

Does this connect with my existing software stack?+

Yes. Automations interface with your existing tools via secure webhooks and REST APIs. We integrate tools like Google Workspace, Slack, HubSpot, Airtable, Stripe, PostgreSQL, Notion, and custom internal systems.

What happens after launch if something changes?+

Systems include dedicated post-launch support to monitor execution logs, verify error-handling routines, and adapt logic as your operational volume grows.

How quickly can we start?+

Because I maintain a strict limit of 2 to 3 new client builds each month, projects usually start within 3 to 5 business days following the audit and scope sign-off.
