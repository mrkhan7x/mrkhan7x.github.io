Data Governance & Security

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Fundamental Commitment: 100% Client Ownership

At MRKHANSERVICES, we engineer enterprise automation systems with zero vendor lock-in. All pipelines, custom scripts, workflow schemas, and proprietary knowledge bases are deployed directly into your own private infrastructure (cloud accounts, private servers, or self-hosted instances).

Zero Model Training: Your private business data, documents, and customer interactions are never used to train public foundation models or third-party AI systems.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver system architecture services:

  • Contact Information: Name, work email address, and company details submitted through our strategy call scheduling form or direct outreach.
  • Project Requirements: Workflow bottlenecks, tech stack components, and operational goals shared during consultations.
  • Technical Telemetry: Standard non-identifying server access logs used solely to ensure website availability and security.

3. How Information Is Used

Information you provide is strictly used to:

  • Evaluate and design custom automation architectures for your business.
  • Schedule and host agreed-upon 30-minute AI strategy sessions.
  • Deliver technical proposals, statement of work (SOW) documents, and system specifications.

4. Data Sovereignty & Protection

We implement strict security practices. System credentials, API tokens, and private server access shared during active development sprints are stored in encrypted vaults, never shared, and promptly rotated or revoked upon project handoff.

5. Contact

For any questions regarding our data privacy practices or system security audits, contact us directly at info@mrkhanservices.com.
