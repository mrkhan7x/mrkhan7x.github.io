Engineering depth meets practical automation.

I am Muhammad Roman Khan, a systems builder studying BS Artificial Intelligence. My work focuses entirely on replacing manual operational friction with resilient, autonomous background systems.

"A business scales sustainably when skilled people focus on high-judgment decisions rather than moving rows between screens."

Rather than stitching superficial tools together, I ground my work in formal machine learning principles and data engineering. Understanding algorithm mechanics and vector spaces allows me to build systems that select the right model, ground every response in verified documents, and fail gracefully when inputs fall outside boundaries.

Every workflow is engineered around deterministic schemas: APIs, webhooks, and structured databases that eliminate human error. When a system goes live, it runs independently in the background, giving operating teams their hours back.
