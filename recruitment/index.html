Four-step intake execution.

How incoming resumes transform into structured applicant records and instant recruiter alerts.

stage 01 listener node

Webhook Ingestion

Applications from website career portals, job boards, or forms post directly to a secure endpoint in real time with zero manual data entry.

input: multipart form data & resume files
stage 02 langchain extractor

AI Competency Extraction

Structured evaluation extracts technical skills, leadership history, and calculate an objective fit score against required role rubrics.

schema: strict JSON with typed attributes
stage 03 sheets & ats sync

Centralized Ledger Sync

Applicant profile, extracted attributes, score, and timestamps append automatically to the hiring database, creating a clean audit trail.

destination: Google Sheets & ATS webhooks

Qualified applicants trigger a Slack message with review links, while automated confirmation delivers to the candidate inbox.

speed: dispatched in under five seconds
